# Score credits

The submission includes Paganini Sonata Op. 3 No. 6, Carulli Vals, Carcassi Op. 60 No. 11, Bach Bourrée BWV 996, and Carulli La sautillante. Artifact identities and digests are retained in the catalog and companion provenance files.

The four added scores come from **PDMX: A Large-Scale Public Domain MusicXML Dataset for Symbolic Music Processing**, v9, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.15571083](https://zenodo.org/records/15571083). Dataset attribution is under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Individual score catalog rows and source metadata record [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); those declarations are retained separately from the dataset license. Companion provenance includes source metadata, composer/arranger information and source hashes. Raw source-audit snapshots are retained privately by the author and are not needed to build this submission.

Vals, Carcassi No. 11 and Bach retain their source JSON. La sautillante is a derived TAB-staff extraction with standard tuning, repeat expansion, and eight same-onset/same-pitch/same-string shared-voice attacks coalesced to their longest duration. Its extraction report preserves note references, rests, and measure order. All 148 attacks have validated source string/fret pairs. Unmarked dynamics use MIDI velocity 80. Finger assignments are not inferred. Paganini retains its exact compressed artifact and recorded CC0 provenance.

Playback uses the attributed GeneralUser GS SoundFont. No external musical recording is added. Upstream declarations are recorded as evidence, not a new independent ownership claim.
